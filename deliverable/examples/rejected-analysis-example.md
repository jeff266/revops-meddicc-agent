## Key Lessons from This Rejection

### What Went Wrong

1. **Lazy Evidence:** "Jennifer mentioned she can make decisions" doesn't show what she said
2. **Score Inflation:** 9/10 for "seems supportive" is not justified
3. **Vague Actions:** "Follow up on metrics" isn't executable
4. **Ignored Context:** "Discussed in Call #2" but didn't reference what was learned
5. **Wishful Thinking:** All components trending up (unrealistic)

### What Good Looks Like

1. **Specific Evidence:** "Call #4, 18:32: 'I have budget authority up to $100K'"
2. **Honest Scoring:** 6/10 for "responsive and engaged" (good rapport, not yet advocacy)
3. **Actionable Steps:** "Schedule call with Jennifer by Aug 5 to confirm budget authority"
4. **Context-Aware:** "In Call #2, we learned X. In Call #4, we confirmed Y."
5. **Realistic Trends:** Some components up, some flat, one down (honest assessment)

### How to Avoid Rejection

- **Before submitting:** Read each component and ask "Could I defend this score in a deal review?"
- **Evidence test:** For every claim, can you point to the transcript and say "Here's where they said that"?
- **Action test:** Can someone execute your recommended actions without asking "who?" or "how?"?
- **Consistency test:** Do your Deal Health, Risk Level, and Stage recommendation match your component scores?

**Remember:** The Evaluator is protecting your CRM from garbage data. If you can't defend the analysis with evidence, don't submit it.
